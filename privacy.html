Kto przetwarza dane / Who we are

Lepestok (Instagram: @lepestok.warsaw) is a flower shop in Warsaw. Contact: flowersshop02@proton.me.

Jakie dane / What data we process

When you send us a direct message on Instagram, our internal order tool receives your Instagram username, display name, Instagram-scoped user ID and the text of your messages. If you place an order, we also store the details you give us: name, phone number, delivery address, date and bouquet.

Po co / Why

Only to answer your messages and to prepare and deliver your order. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties, except couriers when delivery is needed.

Gdzie i jak długo / Storage

Data is stored in a private, access-restricted database used only by the shop team. Order data is kept as long as needed for accounting (up to 5 years under Polish law); message history is kept up to 12 months.

Twoje prawa / Your rights (GDPR / RODO)

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to flowersshop02@proton.me or via Instagram direct message. Deletion requests are handled within 30 days.
